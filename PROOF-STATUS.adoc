== Quarantined (1)

`Layout.idr` — RSR-template scaffolding, excluded from the pass-required
set and reported by the gate on every run. Why: three named holes
(`?fieldsAlignedProof`, `?exampleFieldsAligned`, `?offsetInBoundsProof`),
a divisibility "proof" attempted via `Refl` on abstract arithmetic
(unprovable as stated), `verifyAllPlatforms` returning `Right ()` while
checking nothing, and `Nat` subtraction/`Divides` machinery that does not
compile. No module imports it. Disposition (owner): rewrite honestly
against real mod/div lemmas, or delete.

== History — read this before trusting old claims

Until 2026-07-21 these modules had NEVER been run through idris2:

- 5 of 6 failed `--check` outright. `Soundness.idr` referenced `Step`
  before its definition, returned erased implicits relevantly in its
  canonical-forms lemmas, hid two NAMED HOLES in the substitution lemma
  behind a comment insisting they were "not believe_me or postulate" —
  and its `subst` function was semantically WRONG under binders
  (substituted the lambda's own bound variable; the de Bruijn bug is why
  the original proof could not be completed). `LevelMonotonicity.idr`
  had a `Fin 11`-vs-`Fin 10` off-by-one in the `SafeUp` constructor of
  the monotonicity model itself, a circular (uninhabitable) `SafeL1`
  base case, and stdlib lemma names that no longer exist.
- The one thing `--check` DID accept silently was a hole:
  `?featureMonoGeneral` — the central feature-subsumption theorem was
  only proven for L1. Holes pass `idris2 --check`; that is exactly why
  the gate scans for them.
- `applySubst` in InferenceSoundness.idr recursed through the
  substitution's range and was non-terminating on cyclic substitutions;
  it is now single-pass, modelling idempotent (MGU-form) substitutions,
  with the invariant documented.
